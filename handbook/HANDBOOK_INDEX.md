# Engineering Handbook Index

## Welcome

Welcome to my Engineering Handbook.

This repository is the central reference for how I approach software engineering, quality assurance, documentation, continuous learning, and AI-assisted development.

Unlike a traditional portfolio, this handbook focuses less on individual technologies and more on the principles, processes, and decisions that guide my work.

The projects documented throughout this repository are intended to demonstrate engineering judgment rather than simply technical implementation.

This handbook is maintained as a living document and will continue evolving throughout my career.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of this handbook is to:

* Document my engineering philosophy.
* Establish consistent engineering standards.
* Preserve architectural reasoning.
* Capture lessons learned.
* Organize project knowledge.
* Demonstrate professional growth.
* Provide a long-term knowledge base for both human and AI collaboration.

---

# How This Handbook Is Organized

The handbook progresses intentionally from philosophy to implementation.

Each chapter builds upon the previous one.

Understanding the earlier chapters provides important context for the projects documented later.

The recommended reading order is the same as the chapter order.

This handbook is intended to evolve alongside my engineering career.

* New projects are completed.

Every engineer develops habits, principles, and workflows over time.

This handbook documents mine.
