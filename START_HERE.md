# START HERE

Welcome to my Engineering Handbook.

This repository serves as the central reference for how I approach software engineering, quality assurance, documentation, and continuous learning.

Unlike a traditional portfolio, this handbook focuses less on individual technologies and more on the principles, processes, and decisions that guide my work. It documents not only *what* I build, but *why* I build it that way.

---

# Purpose

This handbook exists to:

* Document my engineering philosophy.
* Maintain consistent technical standards.
* Capture important design decisions.
* Organize project knowledge.
* Record lessons learned.
* Provide a foundation for future growth.

It is intended to evolve throughout my career.

---

# Intended Audience

This handbook is written for:

* Future versions of myself
* Collaborators
* Hiring managers
* Recruiters
* Technical interviewers
* AI assistants supporting my work

Each audience should be able to understand how I think about engineering, even if they have never seen my projects before.

# Guiding Philosophy

Several themes appear throughout this handbook:

* Simplicity over unnecessary complexity.
* Understand the business before designing the solution.
* Quality begins long before testing.
* Documentation is part of the software.
* Continuous improvement is more valuable than perfection.
* AI should enhance engineering judgment, not replace it.

These principles influence every project described here.

---

# Living Document

This handbook is intentionally maintained as a living document.

As new projects are completed, technologies evolve, or better approaches are discovered, this handbook will be updated to reflect those changes.

Older ideas are not removed simply because they have changed; when appropriate, they are revised with additional context so that the evolution of the thinking remains understandable.

# Repository Structure

This repository is organized into chapters, supporting documentation, reusable templates, and reference material.

Each chapter focuses on a specific aspect of my engineering practice while collectively forming a complete picture of how I approach software development and quality assurance.

---

# For AI Collaborators

If you are an AI assistant helping with my projects:

* Treat this handbook as the authoritative source for my engineering philosophy and professional preferences.
* Follow the documented standards whenever possible.
* Preserve consistency with existing terminology and design decisions.
* Explain reasoning, assumptions, and tradeoffs rather than only producing solutions.
* Recommend improvements when they align with the principles documented here.
* If you identify contradictions or outdated guidance, point them out rather than silently overriding them.

The goal is collaboration, not automation.
